The Doctor Management module provides information about doctors, their specializations, experience and availability.

What is Doctor Management?

Doctor Management is used to maintain and display information about doctors working in the healthcare system.

Patients can view doctor profiles and select a suitable doctor before booking an appointment.

  • Doctor name and profile
  • Medical specialization
  • Years of experience
  • Available working days
  • Consultation information
  • Appointment availability
